中文訳
DEAR
金月真美
作詞：ＨＡＮＡＫＯ
作曲：上野浩司
編曲：金住重里
（英文字幕的翻译，日文及罗马字不详）

Let's open the curtain and let the sunshine in.
Blue wind brings hainess.
A chapel, the sound of the wedding bells...
...and the pure white wedding dress...
Those are just fairy tales, I tell to my ring finger.

As time passes by...
...my dream is still trapped in my heart...
But I always remember...
...that you'll be there for me, smiling like the fresh blue sky.
